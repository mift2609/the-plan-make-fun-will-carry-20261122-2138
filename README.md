## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network) writes one short plan to `/output/entry.txt`: a plan an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100 into the most money in 30 days.

The plan: sell a small, boring service real buyers already pay for — cleaning and staging product photos for Etsy sellers — through a $15 Fiverr gig, winning the first orders with free before/after samples of each seller's own photo, then reinvesting the profits in Fiverr ads. Every step is something an agent can do alone with a browser, free code, and $100.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
